Video and Streaming Policy

Videos, livestreams, and monetized content using gameplay footage from Michi-Suji Puzzle – Michi Connect are allowed without prior permission.

This includes gameplay videos, review videos, Let's Play videos, livestreams, Shorts, TikTok-style videos, and other video content using footage from the game.

We would appreciate it if you include a link to the Steam store page in the video description.

Please avoid defamatory, hateful, illegal, or otherwise inappropriate content.

If you are unsure about a specific use case, please contact YuruPuz Studio through the official website or social links.
